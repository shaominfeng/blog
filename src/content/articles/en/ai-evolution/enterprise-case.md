> How the AI platform at my company actually evolved

## Project Overview

This is an internal AI service platform at my company. It ran from a POC in 2023 to maturity in 2026, and passed through every stage of the AI technology curve along the way.

## Core Milestones

### 2023: Infrastructure Build-Out
- **2023.02** - Project starts as a POC
- **2023.04** - API Gateway v1.0 goes live

### 2024: Service Layer and Opening Up
- **2024.04** - Portal opens, 4000+ users
- **2024.06.20** - AI Endpoint goes live
- **2024.07** - Private model v1, first integration
- **2024.09** - Private model v2 (2.5x performance)
- **2024.10** - OIDC platform support

### 2025: Ecosystem and Cost Optimization
- **2025.02.06** - DeepSeek-R1 integrated fast (17 days after release)
- **2025.06.19** - Dify in production
- **2025.11** - Playground MCP (OpenWebUI+MCP)
- **2025.12** - Enterprise subscription (costs under control)

### 2026: Practice Validated
- **2026.01** - Skills ROI validated (10-15 hours saved per week)

## Key Takeaways

1. **Fast follow**: new tech ships → integrated within 1-2 weeks (vs. 3-6 months industry-wide)
2. **Own models**: private models, data privacy under our control
3. **Incremental evolution**: 2023 infrastructure → 2024 services → 2025 ecosystem → 2026 practice
4. **Technical validation**: Skills spread faster than MCP (20+ vs 10+), which confirms what this series argues
5. **Cost management**: from early cost lessons to a subscription model, a complete management system
